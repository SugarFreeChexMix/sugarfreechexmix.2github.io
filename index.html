Wordle

How To Play

Find groups of four words that share something in common.

  • Tap four words, then hit Submit.
  • Each wrong guess costs one of your 4 mistakes. You'll see "One away..." when 3 of your 4 words belong together.
  • Each group has a color. Yellow is the easiest, then green, blue, and purple is the trickiest.
  • Watch out for words that could fit in more than one group.

Unlocked Mode (beta): turn it on in settings. One word in each group is locked. Tap it to play a mini-game (Wordle, Anagram, Word Search or Crossword) and open it.

There's no daily limit. Hit Next Puzzle when you finish for a new one.

How To Play

Fill the grid so every row, column and 3x3 box has the numbers 1 to 9 once.

  • Tap a square, then tap a number. You can also use your keyboard.
  • A wrong number turns red and adds a mistake. Erase it and try again.
  • Turn on Notes to jot small pencil marks instead of a number.
  • Stuck? Hint fills in one square for you.

Keys: 1-9 place a number, arrows move, Backspace erases, N toggles notes, U undoes, H gives a hint.

How To Play

Fill the little crossword using the clues. Words run across and down.

  • Tap a square to pick it. Tap it again to switch between across and down.
  • Type with the keyboard below or your own keyboard.
  • Check marks any wrong letters in red. Reveal fills in the whole word you're on.
  • The timer starts right away, so go fast!

Keys: arrows move, Space or Tab switches clues, Backspace erases.

How To Play

Make words from the six letters. Find the word that uses all of them to win.

  • Tap letters or type to build a word. Words need 4 or more letters.
  • Press Enter to try it. Each letter can be used once.
  • The bar fills as you find the common words. Rare words still count as a bonus.
  • Hint shows the start of a word you haven't found.

Keys: letters type, Enter tries the word, Backspace deletes, Space shuffles.

How do I make a token?
  1. On GitHub, go to Settings, then Developer settings, then Personal access tokens, then Fine-grained tokens.
  2. Tap Generate new token and give it any name.
  3. Under Repository access, pick Only select repositories and choose this repo.
  4. Under Permissions, set Contents to Read and write.
